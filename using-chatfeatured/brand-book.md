# Brand Book

> Your project's identity, description and customer personas, and how ChatFeatured uses them

The Brand Book is where ChatFeatured keeps what it knows about your project: its name, domain, a description of what you do, your industry, your product categories, and the customer personas you're trying to reach. The agent, article writing and prompt suggestions all read from it.

Open it from **Brand > Brand Book** in the sidebar. It needs an active or trialing plan. Viewers can read it but not edit it.

## Create your brand book

If the project has no brand book yet, the page shows **Create Your Brand Book**. Click **Generate Brand Book** and ChatFeatured analyzes your website at the project's domain. It writes the brand book and one customer persona, both of which you can edit afterwards.

Projects without a real website domain can't generate one. Fill in the form instead: **Short Description** (required), **Description**, **Industry** and **Product/Service Categories**, then click **Save Brand Book**.

### Identity

* **Logo:** upload a JPEG, PNG, GIF or WebP under 2 MB.
* **Brand Name** and **Domain:** click the pencil to edit them. Changing either affects how mentions of your project are matched in AI answers, so you're asked to confirm first.
* **Default Country:** the tracking country used when new prompts are added.
* **Name Variations:** alternative names or spellings used to detect your project in source pages. Add abbreviations, old names or common misspellings.

Identity changes save as soon as you click **Done**, add a variation or upload a logo.

### Description

* **Short Description:** a brief summary of what you do.
* **Description:** a fuller description of your products and services.
* **Industry:** for example "Automotive Manufacturing - Hybrid & Electric Vehicles".
* **Product/Service Categories:** type a category and click **Add**.

These fields don't save on their own. Click **Save Changes** in the bar at the bottom, or **Cancel** to discard them.

## The Personas tab

A persona describes a type of customer. Each has a **Name**, **Description**, **Age Range** and **Education Level**.

* Click **Add Persona**, fill in the fields, then click **Create Persona**.
* Click the pencil on a persona to edit it, then click **Save**.
* To delete a persona, click the pencil, then the trash icon next to **Save**. Prompts linked to it stay, they just lose the link.

## How ChatFeatured uses it

* **The agent** reads the brand book before answering questions about your project. See [The ChatFeatured agent](../ai-agents/overview.md).
* **Article writing** uses your project name, domain, industry and short description as brand context for every article. Voice and writing rules belong in [Content Hub > Writing Guidelines](./articles.md#writing-guidelines), not in the brand book.
* **Prompt suggestions** under **Prompt Tracker > Suggestions** are written for each persona: the searches that persona would type when looking for products like yours. More accurate personas give you more relevant suggestions. See [Prompts](./prompts.md).
* **Mention matching** uses your brand name and domain to find your project in AI answers. Name variations help detect it in the pages those answers cite.

You can also ask the agent to update the brand book or manage personas, for example "add a persona for procurement managers at mid-size companies".
