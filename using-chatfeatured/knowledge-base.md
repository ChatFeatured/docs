# Knowledge base

> Upload documents, pages and notes about your company so the agent and the article writer get your facts right

The knowledge base holds documents about your company: product sheets, pricing pages, case studies, certifications, internal notes. The ChatFeatured agent and the article writer read it when they need facts about you. Open it from **Brand > Knowledge Base** in the sidebar. The page is titled **Agent Knowledge Base**.

It needs an active or trialing plan. Viewers can browse it but not change it.

## What it's used for

* **Article writing.** The knowledge base is the source of truth for facts about your company, such as pricing, certifications, customers and product capabilities. If web research disagrees with your documents, the writer goes with your documents. If your documents don't support a claim about your company, the writer leaves it out.
* **The agent.** In Chat, the agent reads your documents when you ask about your own company or mention a document.
* **The article editor.** In the editor's agent panel, type `@` to pull in a document or a whole folder.

Use the [Brand Book](./brand-book.md) for voice, audience and positioning, and the knowledge base for source material and facts.

## Add documents

Click **Add Document**. The dialog has three tabs.

> [tab] File
>
> Drag files onto the drop zone or click it to pick them, then click **Add File**. You can also drop files anywhere on the Documents tab.
>
> Supported formats: `epub`, `pdf`, `docx`, `txt`, `html`, `md`. Up to 21 MB each.

> [tab] URL
>
> Paste one or more URLs, separated by new lines or commas, then click **Add URL**.
>
> Each URL is read as a single page. Links on that page aren't followed, so add every page you want.
>
> ![The URL tab of the Add Document dialog, with two URLs pasted in](https://mintcdn.com/chatfeatured/6uBafkjCiCK9CA0J/images/knowledge-base/add-url.webp?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=6uBafkjCiCK9CA0J\&q=85\&s=269092a8e25731fb420e754ffd0b0327)

> [tab] Text
>
> Enter a **Document title** and paste the content, then click **Add Text**. Use this for notes that don't live anywhere else.

Each document shows a status: **Pending**, **Processing**, **Indexed** (ready for the agent and writer) or **Failed**. If a document fails, delete it and add it again.

## Organize documents

Search the Documents tab by name, or filter by type (Files, URLs, Text).

* **New Folder** creates a folder. Use the pencil icon on a folder to rename it.
* **Move to folder**, in a document's **⋯** menu, moves it into a folder or back to **No folder**. Select several documents to move or delete them together.
* Click the pencil next to a document's title to rename it. You can't edit a document's content after it's added. To change it, delete it and add the new version.

> [!WARNING]
>
> Deleting a folder deletes every document in it.

## Choose what an article uses

By default, an article uses your whole knowledge base. To narrow it, open the article idea before you approve it. Under **Knowledge Base (Help the writing agent with brand context)**:

* The **Enabled / Disabled** switch turns the knowledge base on or off for this article.
* **Select documents or folders to focus on...** picks specific documents or folders.

## Rules tab

Rules for the agent are now its writing rules, the **Rules** tab of **Content Hub > Writing Guidelines**. The **Rules** tab links there with **Open writing rules**. See [Writing rules](./articles.md#writing-rules).
