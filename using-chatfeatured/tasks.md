# Tasks

> Track the work that comes out of ChatFeatured: article reviews, site audit fixes, product page fixes and agent opportunities

Tasks is one list of the work your team has to do in this project. Open it from **Tasks → All tasks** in the sidebar.

## Where tasks come from

Only content tasks are created for you. Everything else exists because someone on your team added it.

| Type | Created | How |
| - | - | - |
| Content | Automatically | As articles move through the workflow (see below) |
| Site audit | By you | **Assign** on an issue, or **Add all issues** / **Add all** in [Site Audit](./site-audit.md) |
| Product audit | By you | **Assign Task** on a check in [Product Page Audit](./product-page-audit.md) |
| Opportunity | By you | **Add to Tasks** on an opportunity the agent surfaces, or by asking the agent to add it |

Content tasks are created when:

* an article idea is saved ("Review suggested article: ...")
* a draft finishes writing and is ready for review ("Article draft is ready for review")
* an article is scheduled ("Publish Scheduled Article")
* an article is ready to sync to Framer ("Sync to Framer CMS")

## The list

![The Tasks list, showing open site audit tasks with their priority, status, assignee and due date](https://mintcdn.com/chatfeatured/6uBafkjCiCK9CA0J/images/tasks/tasks-list.webp?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=6uBafkjCiCK9CA0J\&q=85\&s=3810e495bea1ae5fdc422e741bf1407c)

**Open** holds open and in-progress tasks. **Completed** holds completed and dismissed ones.

Above the tabs, three pills filter with one click:

* **Overdue:** past the due date.
* **Ready to review:** article drafts waiting for review.
* **Due soon:** due within the next 3 days.

You can also search, filter by type, and filter by assignee (**All users**, **My queue**, **Unassigned**, or a teammate).

Click a row to open what it's about: the issue in Site Audit, the article (or the Content Calendar if it's scheduled), the check in the product page audit, or the opportunity's detail page.

## Update a task

Click a cell to change it:

* **Priority:** High, Medium or Low.
* **Status:** Open, In Progress, Completed or Dismissed.
* **Assignee:** any teammate with access to this project, or Unassigned.
* **Due:** pick a date, or **Clear date**.

Priority, assignee and due date lock once a task is completed or dismissed. Status stays editable, so you can reopen it.

### Approve from the list

The **⋯** menu on an open content task offers one approval action, depending on where the article is:

* **Approve draft** (draft or in review): approves the article and closes the task.
* **Approve & generate** (still an idea): approves the idea and starts writing. This uses one monthly article credit.
* **Mark complete** (anything else): closes the task.

The same menu has **Dismiss**.

### Tasks that close themselves

* A site audit task completes when a re-audit finds the issue fixed.
* A content task completes when its draft is approved.

## Opportunity tasks

An opportunity's detail page shows the opportunity, a timeline, task details and **Quick Actions**: **Mark Complete** and **Dismiss**. Content opportunities also get **Generate Article**, which hands the task to the agent: it creates the article idea, starts writing (using one monthly article credit) and closes the task.

## Task settings and email

Click **Task settings** at the top right of the page. For each task type (Content, Opportunity, Site audit, Product audit) you can set:

* **Auto-assign to:** who new tasks of that type go to. "No one" leaves them unassigned.
* **Email:** whether that person gets an email when a task of that type is created.

When you assign a task to a teammate, they get an in-app notification and an email, unless they've turned off **Task Assignments** under Notification Preferences in their Profile settings.
