# Product page audit

> Score a single product page on how well AI answers can read, trust and recommend it

Product Page Audit checks one product page at a time, on your own store or on a retailer such as Amazon, Walmart or Target, and tells you what stops AI answers from recommending it. Open it from **Website → Product Page Audit** in the sidebar.

It's different from [Site Audit](./site-audit.md), which crawls your whole site and checks its technical health. Product Page Audit reads the product content itself: the description, specs, reviews, policies and so on.

It needs an active or trialing plan. Viewers can open past results but not run audits.

## Monthly limit

Each workspace can run 25 product page audits per billing period, on every plan, shared across all its projects. **Re-audit** on an existing audit doesn't count toward the 25.

When you're out, starting an audit fails with "You've reached your product page audit limit. Upgrade your plan for more audits."

## Run an audit

**1. Paste a product page URL**

Enter the full URL of the product page and click **Run Audit**.

**2. Wait for the results**

The audit shows **In Progress** in the history table while it fetches and analyzes the page.

**3. Open the result**

Click the audit in **Audit History** to see its scores and checks.

## What it checks

There are 22 checks in three categories, each scored out of 100.

| Category | Checks |
| - | - |
| Content Quality | Product Description Quality (150+ words), Specific Claims & Data, Descriptive Product Title, Key Features Listed, Measurable Specifications, Use Cases Mentioned, Natural Readability |
| AEO / AI Citability | Direct Factual Answers, Q\&A Formatted Content, Comparison Data Points, Consistent Information, Content Freshness Signals, Unique Value Proposition, Content Completeness, Category Context |
| Trust & Credibility | Customer Reviews Present, Rating Displayed, Return Policy Visible, Shipping Info Visible, Warranty Information, Certifications Mentioned, Seller/Brand Identity |

The overall score weights Content Quality at 35%, AEO at 35% and Trust & Credibility at 30%.

## Read the results

The result page shows the three category scores and the overall score. Below that, checks are split into **Issues** and **Passed** tabs.

Each failing check explains **Why it matters for AI** and **How to fix**. Use **Assign Task** to send it to a teammate as a [task](./tasks.md).

On the main page, the gauges at the top average each category across all completed audits. **Audit History** lists every audit with its retailer, scores and status.

Click **Re-audit** on a result to check the page again after you've made changes.
