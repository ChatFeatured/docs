# Index submissions

> Submit your site to AI search engines directly, every week and whenever you publish

Index Submissions submits your site to AI search engines directly, so new and changed pages get picked up sooner. It lives under **Website → Index Submissions** in the sidebar.

You verify that you own the site once, by hosting a small key file or adding a redirect. After that ChatFeatured submits your pages for you:

* **Right after verification.** ChatFeatured finds your pages from your sitemap and internal links and submits them.
* **Every Sunday at 00:00 UTC.** It finds your pages again and resubmits the full list, so pages you added outside ChatFeatured are covered too.
* **When you publish.** An article published through ChatFeatured to a verified site is submitted as soon as it goes live.

Submitting a page asks for it to be crawled. It doesn't guarantee the page gets indexed or cited in AI answers.

Index Submissions needs an active or trialing plan.

## Limits

* **2 websites per project.** Sites pending verification count too. Delete a site to free a slot, or contact us for more.
* **One host per site.** `www.example.com` and `example.com` count as the same site, but `blog.example.com` is a separate one.

## Common questions

> [accordion] Do I have to verify again later?
>
> No, but keep the key file or redirect in place. Every weekly run checks it again before submitting, and the run fails if the file is gone.

> [accordion] Can I submit a single page by hand?
>
> Not from this page. **Resubmit now** in a site's menu resubmits the whole site. To submit one URL, open the page in [Site Audit](../using-chatfeatured/site-audit.md) and use **Request indexing** (shown when Search Console is connected and the site is verified here).

> [accordion] I use WordPress. Do I need to do anything?
>
> Connecting the [WordPress integration](../integrations/wordpress.md) sets up Index Submissions for that site and verifies it through the plugin. It counts toward the 2-site limit.

>
> Add your website, verify it, and read the submission history.
