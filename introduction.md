# Introduction

> ChatFeatured shows how AI answer engines talk about your brand, and helps you change what they say.

ChatFeatured asks AI answer engines the questions your buyers ask, every day, and records what comes back: whether your brand is named, which competitors are named instead, and which pages the answers cite. Then it helps you act on it. You can write and publish articles, audit your site for AI crawlers, and submit your site to AI search engines directly.

## How it works

1. **You choose the prompts.** A prompt is a question a buyer might ask an AI engine, like "best CRM for small agencies". ChatFeatured suggests a first set during signup, and you edit it.
2. **ChatFeatured runs them daily** on each engine your plan includes, in each country you track.
3. **You read the results.** Visibility, sentiment, citations and competitor rankings, by prompt, topic, engine and country.
4. **You act on the gaps.** The agent suggests articles and writes them, the site audit finds pages AI crawlers can't read, and index submissions put new pages in front of AI search engines.

## Where things are in the dashboard

![The dashboard home page, with Brand performance, Tasks, Week over week by model and a Visibility by model chart](https://mintcdn.com/chatfeatured/6uBafkjCiCK9CA0J/images/dashboard/home.webp?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=6uBafkjCiCK9CA0J\&q=85\&s=560f77edbcb8617b00bcc24d4ba616ed)

* **Home**: this week against last week for your project.
* **Agent**: chat with the ChatFeatured agent about your data, and review what it remembers. See [AI agent](./ai-agents/overview.md).
* **Data Analytics**: visibility trends, competitor rankings and cited sources. See [Analytics](./using-chatfeatured/analytics.md).
* **Prompt Tracker**: your prompts, [topics and keywords](./using-chatfeatured/topics-and-keywords.md), and suggestions. See [Prompts](./using-chatfeatured/prompts.md).
* **Website**: [Website Analytics](./using-chatfeatured/website-analytics.md), [Agent Analytics](./using-chatfeatured/agent-analytics.md) (AI crawler visits), [Site Audit](./using-chatfeatured/site-audit.md), [Index Submissions](./index-submissions/index-submissions.md) and [Product Page Audit](./using-chatfeatured/product-page-audit.md).
* **Content Hub**: [articles and the Content Calendar](./using-chatfeatured/articles.md), the [writing guidelines](./using-chatfeatured/articles.md#writing-guidelines) and [research rules](./using-chatfeatured/articles.md#research) every article follows, and the [Blog CMS](./integrations/blog-cms.md), a blog ChatFeatured hosts for you.
* **Brand**: your [Brand Book](./using-chatfeatured/brand-book.md), [Knowledge Base](./using-chatfeatured/knowledge-base.md) and [Competitors](./using-chatfeatured/competitors.md).
* **Ads**: ChatGPT ads. See [ChatGPT Ads](./using-chatfeatured/chatgpt-ads.md).
* **Automations**: agent work that runs on a schedule. See [Automations](./using-chatfeatured/automations.md).
* **Tasks**: the to-do list for your team and the agent. See [Tasks](./using-chatfeatured/tasks.md).
* **Integrations**: publishing to your CMS, website analytics, AI crawler logs, the API and MCP. See [Integrations](./integrations/overview.md).
* **Settings**: workspace, members, projects, billing and your profile.

Agency workspaces also get an **Agency** section. See [Agency workspaces](./account/agency.md).
