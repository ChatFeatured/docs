# Quickstart

> Sign up, set up your first project, start your trial, and what to do on day one.

Signing up walks you through a short setup wizard. By the end you have a workspace, your first project, a Brand Book, competitors and the topics your buyers ask AI about, and you start a 7-day trial. You choose your prompts on the Getting Started page right after.

If someone invited you to an existing workspace, open the link in the invitation email instead. See [Accepting an invitation](../account/team-management.md#accepting-an-invitation).

## Create your account

1. Go to [chatfeatured.com/login](https://chatfeatured.com/login) and choose **Start free trial**.
2. Sign up with Google, or with your name, email and a password.
3. If you used email and password, click the link in the verification email.

Sign up with your work email. Personal addresses are refused with "Please use your business email to sign up." unless you were invited.

## Choose how to start

* **I'm a Brand** opens the setup wizard below.
* **I'm an Agency** opens a booking page to talk to our team. See [Agency workspaces](../account/agency.md).

## The setup wizard

**1. About you**

Pick how you heard about ChatFeatured and your role.

**2. Brand**

Fill in:

* **Brand Name**: the name AI answers should mention.
* **Brand Website**: your site's domain, like `example.com`.
* **Primary Market**: the country your prompts are tracked in to start.
* **Content Language**: the language ChatFeatured writes in for this project.

This creates your workspace and first project, and ChatFeatured starts reading your website to build your Brand Book and topics.

**3. Competitors**

Search for competitors and add at least 3.

**4. Brand Book**

ChatFeatured sums up your brand in one sentence: your industry, what you offer and, for a local business, your city and the places you serve. Click any highlighted part to change it. If you're a local business, confirm the city before you continue. The agent and the article writer use this, so it's worth a minute.

**5. Topics**

Topics are the subjects your buyers ask AI about, ranked by how often people search for them on Google. **What you offer** comes from your Brand Book; **Also worth tracking** are related subjects buyers ask about on the way. Click a topic to see example prompts, add your own topic, or remove one with the bin icon.

**6. Models**

Optional. Tell us which AI engines matter most to you. Your plan sets which engines are tracked.

**7. Search Console and Google Analytics**

Optional. Connect [Google Search Console](../integrations/google-search-console.md) and a [Google Analytics](../integrations/google-analytics.md) 4 property, or skip and connect later.

**8. Finish**

Pick Starter or Growth and click **Activate trial** to open checkout. A card is required, and you aren't charged until the 7-day trial ends. During the trial you can generate up to 5 AI articles; every other limit applies in full. See [The free trial](../account/billing.md#the-free-trial).

Once the trial is active, set up your prompts on the Getting Started page. If you'd rather have a guided start, **Book a Call** books an onboarding call instead.

## Your first day

After checkout you land on the **Getting Started** page. You can come back to it any time from **Getting Started** at the bottom of the sidebar.

![The Getting Started page, showing setup progress and the steps for your first day](https://mintcdn.com/chatfeatured/6uBafkjCiCK9CA0J/images/getting-started/getting-started-page.webp?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=6uBafkjCiCK9CA0J\&q=85\&s=2d25560117bb269dbc7a322236bff051)

**1. Set up your prompts**

Click **Set up your prompts**. ChatFeatured opens [Generate prompts](../using-chatfeatured/prompts.md#generate-prompts) with your topics ticked and the number of prompts matched to your free slots. Review them and click **Add N to active prompts**. Nothing runs until you do; after that your prompts run every day.

**2. Connect your CMS**

Click **Connect CMS** to connect the site you'll publish articles to: WordPress, Webflow, Ghost, HubSpot, Wix, Sanity, Framer, Shopify or a Git-based CMS. Or let ChatFeatured host the blog with the [Blog CMS](../integrations/blog-cms.md). See [Integrations](../integrations/overview.md).

**3. Work with your agent**

Four short sessions with the [agent](../ai-agents/overview.md): Prompt Strategy, Writing Guidelines, Content Strategy and Set up Automations. Writing Guidelines opens the [writing guidelines](../using-chatfeatured/articles.md#writing-guidelines) page, where the agent can write them from your website. Content Strategy opens **Articles**, where **Let's get started** suggests articles to write.

**4. Publish your first article**

Approve an article idea and ChatFeatured writes it. Review the draft, then publish or schedule it. See [Articles](../using-chatfeatured/articles.md).

**5. Audit your site**

Click **Start Audit** to check your pages for issues that stop AI crawlers reading them. See [Site audit](../using-chatfeatured/site-audit.md).

**6. Set up Index Submissions**

Click **Set up Indexing** and we submit your site to AI search engines directly. See [Index submissions](../index-submissions/index-submissions.md).

Optional steps on the page: **Invite your teammates** ([Team management](../account/team-management.md)), **Connect Google Search Console**, **Connect Google Analytics** and [Agent Analytics](../using-chatfeatured/agent-analytics.md).

Results show up in **Prompt Tracker** after your prompts' first run. Then add or pause prompts so you're tracking the questions that matter. See [Prompts](../using-chatfeatured/prompts.md).
